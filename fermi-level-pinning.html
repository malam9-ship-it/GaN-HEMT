Where the Fermi level locks in a doped buffer

A 1D Poisson solve of barrier, channel and buffer. Add enough Fe or C to outnumber the residual Si/O donors and EF in the buffer stops tracking the donors. It locks onto the deep acceptor level, 1–2 eV from either band edge, and the buffer becomes semi-insulating.

How to read it

  • Below the donor background, the few deep acceptors fill completely and the leftover donors keep EF within about 0.2 eV of EC. The buffer conducts and gives drain–source leakage and poor pinch-off.
  • Above it, only the fraction ND/NT of traps needs to hold an electron. EF then sits within a few kT of ET, and a factor of 10 more dopant moves it by only 60 meV. That plateau is the pinning.
  • “Midgap” is shorthand. Fe pins EF in the upper half of the gap (EC − 0.6 eV in GaN). C pins it in the lower half (EV + 0.9 eV), so a C-doped buffer is weakly p-type. Either way both n and p fall below about 107 cm−3 in GaN.
  • AlGaN channels widen the gap. If the deep level stays fixed against vacuum (Langer–Heinrich rule), EC rises away from it, so the same dopant pins EF deeper and closer to true midgap.
  • The undoped channel spacer sets how far the ionized traps sit from the 2DEG. Thinning it tightens the back barrier but pushes the trap charge toward the channel, which in real devices means more current collapse.

Model and parameters

Equilibrium, classical 2DEG (Fermi–Dirac statistics, no subband quantization), relaxed buffer with the barrier and channel coherently strained to it. Surface EC − EF is fixed at 0.4·Eg(barrier). The bottom of the buffer is held at bulk neutrality 3 µm below the channel.

AlGaN-channel HEMTs are reported with xch from about 0.1 to 0.85 and barriers 0.15–0.4 richer in Al, up to AlN. Most grow on AlN templates or on C-doped AlGaN buffers. This model has no DX behaviour for Si and O at high x (Gordon et al., PRB 89, 085204 (2014)), which would make the background donors deep and help pinning further. Real Fe profiles also tail into the channel by surface segregation, which this model does not include.
